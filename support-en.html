Dosely — Support

Privacy Policy

Need help with Dosely? Write to us — we answer every message.

Contact us

Email: rnane.1@icloud.com. Please mention your device (iPhone or iPad), your iOS version and what happened. We will reply as soon as we can.

Reminders don’t arrive

In iOS Settings → Notifications → Dosely, make sure notifications are allowed and that a Focus isn’t silencing them. Reminders are scheduled on each device separately, so open Dosely at least once on a new iPhone or iPad.

Sync between iPhone and iPad

Use the same Apple Account on both devices and keep iCloud turned on for Dosely. The sync status is shown in Dosely → Settings. If your iCloud storage is full, sync pauses until you free up space.

Subscription

To get Dosely Plus back on a new device, open Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases. To cancel, go to iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Payments and refunds are handled by Apple.

Your data

In Settings → Your data you can export a backup, export your history as CSV/PDF or delete all data. Your data is never sent to us.

Medical disclaimer

Dosely does not replace a doctor and does not give medical advice. Ask your doctor or pharmacist about your treatment.
