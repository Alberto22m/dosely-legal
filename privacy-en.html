Dosely — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Dosely is a medication and supplement reminder app. This policy explains what happens to your information.

What we store

Your medications, schedules, supplies and dose history are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

No account, no tracking

Dosely has no sign-up, no advertising and no third-party analytics or SDKs. We do not collect, share or sell personal data. The App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.

Notifications and widgets

Reminders are local notifications created on your device. On a paired Apple Watch, iOS mirrors them with the same actions. Widgets read today’s plan from a container shared between the app and its widget on the same device.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details.

Export and deletion

You can export a backup file or a CSV/PDF report at any time and delete all data in Settings → Your data → Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes its data. Backup files you export are under your control.

Children

Dosely is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Medical disclaimer

Dosely does not replace a doctor and does not give medical advice.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the support link on the Dosely App Store page.
