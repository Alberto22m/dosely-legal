Dosely — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Dosely is a medication and supplement reminder app. This policy explains what happens to your information.

What we store

Your medications, schedules, supplies and dose history are stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

iCloud sync

When you are signed in to iCloud, Dosely syncs your data between your iPhone and iPad through the private CloudKit database of your Apple Account. This data is protected by Apple, and we have no servers and no access to it. Without iCloud, everything stays on your device. You can turn sync off in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud.

Medicine search

Name suggestions in the editor come from an offline list built into the app (official open data from openFDA, the French BDPM and the Spanish Ministry of Health, plus Dosely’s own lists). Search runs on your device; what you type is never sent anywhere.

Apple Health

Dosely does not read data from or write data to Apple Health (HealthKit).

No account, no tracking

Dosely has no sign-up, no advertising and no third-party analytics or SDKs. We do not collect, share or sell personal data. The App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.

Notifications, widgets and Siri

Reminders are local notifications created on each of your devices. On a paired Apple Watch, iOS mirrors them with the same actions. Widgets read today’s plan from a container shared between the app and its widgets on the same device. Siri and Shortcuts actions run in the app on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details.

Export and deletion

You can export a backup file or a CSV/PDF report at any time. Settings → Your data → Delete all data removes your data from this device and, with iCloud sync, from your iCloud and your other devices. Deleting the app removes its copy on that device. Backup files you export are under your control.

Children

Dosely is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Medical disclaimer

Dosely does not replace a doctor and does not give medical advice.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the support link on the Dosely App Store page.
